# Close Seal — Provisional Visual Kit

Status: GREYBOX / NOT PRODUCTION ART

The first battlefield intentionally uses procedural Godot primitives rather than committing the project to final character assets.

## Readability language
- Gold: player hero.
- Blue: player frontline force.
- Cyan: player ranged force / allied creeps.
- Violet: player support.
- Red/orange: opposing force.
- Blue/red fortified structures: defensive towers.
- Dark neutral terrain with a lighter central creep lane.

## Purpose
This kit exists to validate camera, scale, silhouette separation, squad spacing, battlefield density, touch readability and the first combat loop before production models are selected.

## Replacement contract
Every primitive role must later be replaceable by a production model without changing authoritative combat simulation. Final models should be mobile-conscious, LOD-ready, silhouette-first, animation-budgeted and provenance/license tracked.

## First production asset slots
1. Hero.
2. Frontline troop.
3. Ranged troop.
4. Support troop.
5. Creep/minion.
6. Defensive tower A.
7. Defensive tower B.
8. Modular terrain/fortification kit.

No primitive in this prototype is final art.
