# Vertical Slice 01

Goal: prove that Close Seal's core mobile battle loop is fun, readable, responsive and technically viable before scaling content.

## Required slice

- 1 hero
- 1 provisional race
- 3 troop archetypes
- 2 defensive tower archetypes
- 1 creep flow
- 1 compact 3D map
- 1 opposing AI force
- touch-first controls
- measurable Android performance

## What this slice must answer

1. Is direct hero control enjoyable on a phone?
2. Can troop commands be issued quickly without desktop-style micromanagement?
3. Are hero, troops, towers and creeps readable at RTS camera distance?
4. Does the battlefield create meaningful tactical decisions?
5. What active-unit budget can representative Android hardware sustain at the target frame rate?
6. Which simulation/rendering architecture should scale into campaign and multiplayer?

## Explicitly out of scope

- four complete races
- full campaign
- ranked service
- production matchmaking
- large progression/economy systems
- final art production

The slice succeeds only if the battle loop and technical foundations are worth expanding.
